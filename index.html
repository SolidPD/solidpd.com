New · Agentic Hardware Development

AI Agents at a Real Test Bench

We put AI agents inside the develop-test loop. They write firmware, flash it to the actual device, drive real lab instruments, read back real measurements, and go around again, around the clock, with a senior engineer in command.

Firmware problems that used to consume weeks of bench time get found, fixed, and verified in days, with every claim backed by a measurement.
